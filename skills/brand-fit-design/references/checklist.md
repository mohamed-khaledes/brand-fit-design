# Checklist

## Kind of business: what visuals fit

| Kind | Examples | Leads with | Allowed extras | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Software | SaaS, AI, dev tool, fintech app | the real product UI as live HTML/screens, showing the actual task | diagrams, one subtle effect, a dark theme | a generic fake dashboard |
| Game | a game, a game studio | its name and world as key art | one effect or scene | an effect replacing the art |
| Hardware / tech product | device, robot, keyboard | photos of the product, big and whole | close-ups of parts, use at the bench | drawn SVG products |
| Physical product / brand | furniture, bikes, coffee online | product photography, in use and in detail | one brand colour band | shaders, glows, mood stock |
| Service | clinic, law firm, agency, trades | people, the work, the rooms | a still diagram of the process | abstract 3D, stock handshakes |
| Place | cafe, restaurant, hotel, shop, gym | many real photographs, the address, the hours | the name or address set large | effects, dark tech looks, loops |

A maker that also opens its door (a bakery with a web shop) is a **place**.

## AI-default looks (allowed only when the brand or references ask for them)

1. Warm cream ground (~#F4F1EA) + high-contrast serif + terracotta/clay accent (~#D97757).
2. Near-black ground with one acid-green or vermilion accent.
3. Broadsheet layout: hairline rules, zero radius, dense newspaper columns.
4. The SaaS card kit: everything in identical rounded cards, one radius everywhere, the same soft
   shadow, gradient washes as decoration.
5. Template chrome: tracked ALL-CAPS eyebrow above every heading; "A · B · C" meta strings;
   "WORD — fragment" labels; #0B0B0B/#111 standing in for black; monospace for small labels; "→"
   appended to every link.
6. One word in the headline italicised or coloured; 01/02/03 numbering on content that isn't a
   sequence; a big-number-small-label stat strip under the hero.
7. Fade-and-slide-up on every section; hover lift on every card whether linked or not.
8. Violet/purple-blue gradients; glassmorphism blobs; glowing orbs on a non-tech business.

## Writing

- The h1 names what is sold and for whom (or where). One concrete fact on the first screen (a
  price, a location, a number the developer gave).
- Headings name things; buttons are a verb and an object ("Book a table", not "Get started").
- The buyer's words, not the system's. Specific beats clever. Sentence case.
- Ban words (unless literal for the trade): elevate, unlock, unleash, empower, seamless, effortless,
  cutting-edge, revolutionary, transformative, holistic, world-class, game-changing, bespoke,
  leverage, robust, powerful, nestled, delve, tapestry, journey (unless travel), ecosystem.
- Stock lines: "In today's fast-paced world", "Look no further", "X made simple", "Ready to
  transform your…?", "Whether you're A or B", "Join thousands of…", "It's not just X, it's Y".
- No invented specifics, quotes, customers, figures or awards. No exclamation marks in body copy, no
  em-dash habit, no emoji bullets.

## Final look (with the screenshots)

- First screen: what is sold, for whom, main action visible, at desktop and phone.
- Reads as the same family as the references; nothing copied.
- The one bold thing is clear; everything else is quiet.
- Equal side edges, one button style pair, one card style, one radius scale.
- Every picture loads; stand-ins are listed for replacement.
- Phone: one column, tappable controls ≥ 44px, no sideways scroll, menu works.
- Keyboard focus visible, reduced motion respected, text contrast AA.
